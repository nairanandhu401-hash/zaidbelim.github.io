Hi, I'm
MOHAMMED ZAID BELIM

A Video Editor and VFX Artist who loves creating cinematic and creative visuals. I work with video editing, motion graphics, visual effects, and 3D design using tools like Premiere Pro, After Effects, Photoshop, and Blender.

Over the years, I've worked on different projects that have helped me grow both creatively and professionally. I'm always learning new skills and trying to improve my work with every project.

My goal is to build my own creative studio and work on bigger projects, including films, commercials, and digital content that people enjoy and remember.

I enjoy bringing ideas to life and creating visuals that tell a story and leave a lasting impression.
